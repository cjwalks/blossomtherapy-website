Accessibility

We want this website to work for everyone.

  • Plain language, with one main action per section
  • No autoplay, pop-ups or flashing content
  • Works with screen readers, keyboard navigation and text resizing
  • Respects your device's "reduce motion" setting

If something doesn't work for you, or you'd like information in another format, email hello@blossomtherapy.au.
