Who we see

Neurodivergent people, young women, people with invisible or fluctuating conditions, and young people who haven't clicked with traditional models. Home visits around Upper Coomera and Pimpama out to Paradise Point, and telehealth.

Blossom Therapy and Supports

Exercise physiology, physiotherapy and disability support. Home visits across the northern Gold Coast, plus telehealth.
