Privacy policy

How we collect, use and protect your information.

Last updated: [date]

Who we are

Blossom Therapy and Supports [ABN to add] provides exercise physiology, physiotherapy and disability support services. We are bound by the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

What we collect

Contact details, date of birth, funding details (such as NDIS or Medicare), health information relevant to your care, referral information, and notes from your sessions.

How we collect it

From you or your carer through our online forms, email, phone or in person, and from your referrer or other health providers with your consent.

How we store it

Client records and online forms are held in Halaxy, our practice management system, which stores data in Australia. This website doesn't store health information.

How we use and share it

To provide and coordinate your care, to claim funding (for example, with the NDIS or your plan manager), and to communicate with your treating team with your consent, or where the law requires it.

Accessing or correcting your information

You can ask to see or correct your information at any time. Email hello@blossomtherapy.au.

Complaints

Contact us first at hello@blossomtherapy.au. If you're not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au). NDIS participants can also contact the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission.
