What we do

We're a small team of exercise physiologists, a physiotherapist and disability support workers. We work with neurodivergent people, young women, and people living with invisible or fluctuating health conditions.

Our approach is paced, collaborative and safety-focused. Support is built around you, not a generic program.

Who we support

Neurodiversity

Neuro-affirming, paced support for autistic and ADHD people, focused on confidence and safety.

Women's health

Support for endometriosis, pelvic pain and hormonal conditions, focused on movement and daily function.

Invisible illness

Chronic pain, fatigue, POTS, MCAS, EDS and similar conditions, with pacing that adapts on the day.

Youth and teens

Gentle, relationship-led support for young people who feel overwhelmed or misunderstood by traditional models.
