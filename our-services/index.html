Our services

Home visits across the northern Gold Coast, and telehealth Australia-wide.

Exercise physiology

Movement and strength programs built around your body, energy and goals. Includes women's health exercise physiology.

Home visits and telehealth

Physiotherapy

Assessment and treatment for injury, pain and movement problems, with the same paced, neuro-affirming approach.

Home visits and telehealth

Disability support work

Practical support with self-care, decision making and daily planning.

NDIS. 3-hour minimum.

Girls' social groups

Small, supportive groups. Maximum 6 per group.

Wattle Crew

For school-aged girls. Fortnightly, Saturday afternoons.

Banksia Crew

For girls who have left school or are homeschooled. Weekly, Thursday afternoons (occasionally Friday).
