About us

A small team doing paced, collaborative, neuro-affirming care.

Our vision

A future where no one is made to feel broken for having a body or mind that works differently. Our care aims to honour individuality, build trust and support growth without pressure or harm.

How we work

  • Paced: we go at a speed that feels safe for you
  • Collaborative: you, your family and your other supports are part of the plan
  • Adaptive: sessions change on the day if your body or energy does
  • In your space: home visits, or telehealth from wherever suits
